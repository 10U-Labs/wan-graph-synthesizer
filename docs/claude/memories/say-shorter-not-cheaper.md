# Say shorter, not cheaper

Nothing in this repository records money. There is no price, no tariff, no monthly charge and no currency in `src/`, `lib/`, `etc/` or `data/`. Every number the synthesizer computes, compares or publishes is a distance in miles: `PhysicalEdge.distance_miles` on each fiber segment, `path_geometry_miles` for a whole path, `target_miles` and `worst_haul_miles` in the coverage report, and `max_backup_route_multiple`, which is a ratio of one mileage to another. So say shorter, or say the fewest fiber miles. "The cheapest path" claims the design compared two prices, and it did not.

This matters beyond tidiness, because the wrong word sends a reader looking for a setting that does not exist. Somebody told the mesh picks the cheapest path will go looking for where the prices are configured, will not find it in `etc/*.yml`, and cannot then tell whether the prices are somewhere else, absent by oversight, or a thing the answer invented. A reader told it picks the shortest path knows what to look at: `distance_miles`, and the sort in whichever function was named.

Money is allowed as a fact about operators, never as a number about a design. An operator pays for every path they hold, which is the whole reason an unneeded path is a defect worth an issue rather than a harmless extra — that is what GitHub issue #59 was about, and saying it is correct. What is not allowed is comparing two paths by price, calling one option the expensive one, or claiming a change saves money. Say what it saves in miles and give the figure: trimming the duplicate paths took AFGSC from 18,454 to 14,923 miles, which is a measurement, where "it saves money" is a guess about a tariff nobody here has.

One module uses "cost" exactly and should keep it. `synthesizer.ceiling` finds a site's independent paths with a minimum-cost maximum flow, and the comment at `ceiling.py:62` states what a cost is there: the miles on an arc of the residual network. Inside that module, cost, cheapest and refund are the algorithm's own words for mileage, and `_cheapest_runs` is a name a reader can open. Leave them. What must not happen is the word leaking out of the flow and into prose about a finished design, where nobody has told the reader that cost means miles.

Prose written before this was settled says cheapest in several places — `backbone.py:439`, `backbone.py:536`, `backbone.py:660`, `backbone.py:709`, `backbone.py:759` and `backbone.py:833`. Match this rule rather than the docstring next to you, and leave the old text alone unless the file is being changed for some other reason.

This was written on 2026-08-17, after an explanation of how paths are drawn said "the cheapest fiber available" three times running and had to be corrected. The words for the parts of a backbone are in [say-peers-and-paths](say-peers-and-paths.md); naming the identifier a reader can open rather than describing it is [write-the-exact-name](write-the-exact-name.md).
